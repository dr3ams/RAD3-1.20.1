# Stamina in Combat

Stamina is a shared resource between movement, parkour, gliding, and combat. Running it dry in a fight means losing access to your most important tools exactly when you need them most.

## What drains stamina in combat

- **Sprinting** toward or away from enemies
- **Combat rolls** — each roll costs a chunk of stamina
- **Shield Slam** — the dash component costs stamina
- **Melee attacks** — each swing has a small stamina cost
- **ParCool actions** — wall jumps, break falls, and similar moves used in combat

## What your nourishment tier changes

Your [Nourishment tier](../nourishment/four-tiers.md) directly modifies stamina behaviour in combat:

| Tier | Effect |
|---|---|
| Starving | Stamina regen heavily reduced, melee and sprint costs significantly increased |
| Fed | No stamina bonuses or penalties |
| Well-Fed | Idle stamina regen improved, melee cost reduced |
| Peak | Stamina regen significantly improved, sprint and melee costs both reduced |

Entering a dungeon while Starving is a significant handicap. You will drain your stamina faster and recover it slower — meaning fewer rolls, fewer dashes, and less mobility when things go wrong.

## Managing stamina mid-fight

- **Do not sprint constantly** — sprint only to close distance or escape, then stop.
- **Let stamina regen between engagements** — standing still briefly while blocking regenerates stamina faster than sprinting.
- **Eat before you go** — a Peak-tier meal before a dungeon run is one of the highest-value preparations you can make.
