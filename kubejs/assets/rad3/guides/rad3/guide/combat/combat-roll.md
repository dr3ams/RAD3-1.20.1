# Combat Roll

Press **R** (default) to roll in any direction you are moving. You can dodge forwards, backwards, or sideways — the roll follows your movement input, not where you are looking.

## Cooldown

The roll has a **4 second cooldown** by default. The cooldown timer is visible on your HUD next to your hotbar, so you always know when it is available.

Rolling costs hunger, so staying fed matters even in combat — not just for the stat bonuses.

## Improving your roll

The roll cooldown and effectiveness can be improved through several systems:

- **Nourishment tier** — Well-Fed and Peak tiers reduce roll cooldown. Starving significantly increases it.
- **Passive Skill Tree** — several nodes reduce cooldown or improve roll distance and speed.
- **Armor enchantments** — dedicated enchantments exist for roll distance, roll speed, and cooldown reduction. Check JEI for roll-related enchantment books.

## How to use it

The roll grants brief invincibility frames and repositions you quickly. Use it to:

- Dodge a swing you cannot block in time
- Create distance from a mob that has cornered you
- Reposition around an enemy to attack from a better angle

The roll is not a panic button — it has a cooldown long enough that wasting it on nothing will leave you exposed when you actually need it. Treat it as a deliberate resource, not a reflex.
