# Enemy Scaling

Enemies in RAD3 scale dynamically with your progress through **L2 Hostility**. As you play, mobs gain levels, grow stronger, and develop special traits — abilities that change how they fight and how you need to respond.

## How difficulty works

Every mob has a level determined by several factors: the dimension it is in, the biome, its distance from world origin, and your personal difficulty rating. Your personal difficulty rises when you kill mobs and falls when you die. Chunk sections also accumulate their own difficulty from the mobs killed in them.

The result: the world gets harder the more you fight in it. Exploring fresh terrain generally means lower-level mobs than areas where you have been killing things for hours.

## Mob level effects

As mob level increases:
- Higher base health and armor
- Higher damage output
- Higher chance of carrying powerful traits

Mobs display their level and traits visually — look for indicators above their health bar.

## Safe zones

The **Hostility Orb** permanently nullifies mob difficulty in a chunk section. Use it around your base to keep home safe while the rest of the world keeps scaling. Equip **Detector Glasses** before placing it. The **Hostility Detector** item lets you check current difficulty in any area.

The **Curse of Sloth** curio is a softer option — it pauses your personal difficulty from rising, at the cost of also pausing special loot drops.

## Mob Traits

Traits are special abilities that appear on leveled mobs. Regular traits can appear at any level. Advanced and Legendary traits require higher levels or specific conditions to unlock.

### Regular Traits

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Fiery** | Ignites you on hit or when attacking |
| **Invisible** | Mob and its equipment become invisible — use Detector Glasses to see it |
| **Protection** | Gains damage resistance |
| **Regenerate** | Slowly regenerates health |
| **Speedy** | Moves at extreme speed |
| **Tank** | Greatly increased health and armor |
| **Shulker** | Fires shulker bullets periodically; inflicts potion effects instead of levitation |
| **Gravity** | Increases gravity for nearby mobs and players — fall faster, jump lower |
| **Moonwalk** | Decreases gravity for nearby mobs and players — fall slower, jump higher |
| **Counter Strike** | After being hit, dashes back toward the attacker |

### Potion Traits

These mobs apply their named effect on hit: **Blinder** (Blindness), **Distorter** (Nausea), **Levitater** (Levitation), **Stray** (Slowness), **Weakener** (Weakness), **Poisonous** (Poison), **Withering** (Wither), **Cursed**, **Soul Burner**, **Freezing**.

### Advanced Traits
*Appear only after level 100.*

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Adaptive** | Memorises damage types it has taken and becomes resistant to them |
| **Reflect** | Reflects direct melee damage — use ranged or magic |
| **Grenade** | Fires explosive projectiles periodically |
| **Growth** | Slime only — grows larger each time it is healed to full HP |
| **Split** | Splits into two half-level copies with the same traits when killed |
| **Drain** | Removes your positive potion effects and extends your negative ones; bonus damage if you have debuffs |
| **Reprint** | Copies your equipment enchantments on hit and deals bonus damage based on your enchantment total |
| **Corrosion** | Appears after level 200 — drains equipment durability based on how much durability has already been lost. Counter with the Hardened enchantment |
| **Erosion** | Appears after level 200 — drains equipment durability based on remaining durability. Counter with Hardened |

### Legendary Traits
*Unlock only after killing a mob with Rank 5 traits. Appear after level 100.*

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Dementor** | Immune to physical damage; attacks bypass your armor |
| **Dispelling** | Immune to magic damage; attacks bypass magic protections; can temporarily disable your enchantments |
| **Teleport** | Teleports to avoid physical hits — use magic |
| **Repelling** | Launches you away; reduced by Insulation enchantment (full set) |
| **Pulling** | Drags you toward it; reduced by Insulation enchantment (full set) |
| **Master** | Summons allies (boss-exclusive trait) |
| **Killer Aura** | After level 300 — periodically damages all nearby targets; range shown by particles |
| **Ragnarok** | After level 600 — seals equipped items or curios on hit; unseal by holding the item |

## The L2 Hostility Progression Loop

Engaging seriously with L2 Hostility unlocks its own crafting and reward track:

- Kill leveled mobs while wearing a **Looting Charm** to collect trait item drops
- Craft **Chaos Ingots** from trait materials — used in most L2 recipes
- Craft the **Curse of Envy** to raise difficulty further and unlock trait item drops from any mob
- Burn **Bottles of Curse** (1/256 chance) to obtain **Hostility Essence** for **Miracle Ingots**
- Craft increasingly powerful curses, rings, and curios using these materials

Four tiers of Looting Charms exist, each unlocking rarer drops. Trait Shops in the questbook let you exchange **Miracle Powder** for specific curio rewards once you have hit the appropriate kill milestones.
