# Shield Mechanics

Shields in RAD3 have two active techniques beyond basic blocking. Using them correctly turns a shield from a passive damage reducer into an offensive tool.

## Shield Slam

**Attack while blocking** to dash forward and slam your shield into the nearest enemy. A successful slam deals damage and **stuns** the target briefly.

Use Shield Slam to:
- Interrupt a mob mid-swing before it hits you
- Create a stun window to land several free attacks
- Push an enemy back when surrounded

The dash covers a short distance — you need to be relatively close to your target for it to connect.

## Parrying

**Blocking at the right moment** opens a brief window during which any incoming hit will stun the attacker, even if the hit lands on you.

Parrying is harder to execute than Shield Slam because the timing window is narrow. Against slower, telegraphed attacks — large mobs, boss swings, illager melee — it becomes a powerful tool. Against fast mob spam it is less reliable.

## Tips

- A stunned enemy cannot attack. Use the stun window from either technique to deal significant damage without trading hits.
- Shield Slam's dash costs stamina. Do not spam it while already low on stamina or you will find yourself unable to roll when you need to.
- Basic blocking still reduces damage normally. Shield Slam and Parry are opt-in tools, not replacements for simply holding up your shield.
