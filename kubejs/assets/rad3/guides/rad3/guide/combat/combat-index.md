# Combat

Combat in RAD3 is more demanding than vanilla Minecraft. Enemies hit harder, scale with your progression, and do not wait for you to decide what to do. You have tools to match them — but they require deliberate use.

This section covers the active combat mechanics available to you: dodging, shield techniques, and how stamina connects everything together.
