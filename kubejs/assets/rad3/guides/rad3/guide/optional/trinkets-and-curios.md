# Trinkets and Curios

RAD3 includes hundreds of curios — equippable items that occupy dedicated slots outside your standard armour and hotbar. Each one provides a unique ability, passive effect, or stat modifier.

## Accessing curio slots

Open your inventory and look for the button near your character model, or press **Alt + E** to open the curio interface directly. Curio slots are separate from armour slots and do not compete with your equipment.

## Obtaining curios

Curios can be:

- Looted from dungeon chests
- Crafted (check JEI for recipes)
- Received as questbook rewards
- Purchased from the Market using quest coins

Relics, Nameless Trinkets, Artifacts, and Unique Accessories are the main curio-providing mods in the pack. Each has a different rarity distribution and thematic focus.

## Cursed curios

A specific category of powerful curios can only be equipped while wearing the [Catastrophe Scroll](catastrophe-scroll.md). These items provide significant advantages that would be unbalanced without the Scroll's difficulty penalties offsetting them.

## Tips

- Check curio tooltips carefully — some have conditional effects, charges, or cooldowns
- Curios with active abilities may require a keybind to activate; check Controls
- Curio slots can be expanded in some cases — check the Curios API interface for your current slot layout
- JEI can show all curio items by searching for their tags or mod name
