# Catastrophe Scroll

The Catastrophe Scroll is a voluntary challenge item. Equipping it makes RAD3 significantly harder. It is entirely optional and designed for players who feel the base difficulty is not enough.

{/* Triple warning — this is intentional and not excessive for this particular item */}

> **Once equipped, the Catastrophe Scroll cannot be removed. Ever.**

## What it does

The Scroll carries **seven curses** that activate as you play through the game normally. Each curse adds a painful debuff to the player. The curses do not all activate at once — they trigger progressively as you reach specific gameplay thresholds, which means the difficulty escalates the further into the game you go.

## Reversing curses with Etchings

Each curse can be countered by equipping the corresponding **Etching**. Etchings are obtained by killing specific bosses in specific ways — not just by defeating them, but by meeting additional conditions during the fight.

Check each Etching in JEI to see its acquisition requirement. Hunting Etchings gives the Catastrophe Scroll a built-in long-term goal structure: the Scroll makes the game harder, and progressing through its content is how you gradually claw that difficulty back.

## Cursed Curios

Equipping the Catastrophe Scroll unlocks access to a category of powerful cursed curio items that cannot be used without it. These provide significant advantages — the Scroll is not purely a handicap for masochists, it is also a key that opens otherwise inaccessible power.

## The questbook confirmation

The questbook quest for the Catastrophe Scroll requires you to check **five confirmation boxes** before the item is awarded. This is intentional. Read each one.
