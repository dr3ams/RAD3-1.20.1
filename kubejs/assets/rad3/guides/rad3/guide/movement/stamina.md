# Stamina

The stamina wheel governs sprinting, ParCool actions, gliding, and several combat moves. It is a shared resource — everything draws from the same pool.

## The stamina wheel

The stamina bar is displayed on your HUD. It depletes as you sprint, perform parkour moves, or use combat abilities, and regenerates automatically when you are not actively draining it.

How quickly it regenerates — and how quickly it drains — is affected by your [Nourishment tier](../nourishment/four-tiers.md). Being well-fed makes a meaningful difference to how long you can sustain movement chains.

## Expanding your stamina

Your stamina pool can be permanently increased using **Stamina Vessels** and **Spirit Orbs**.

Stamina Vessels are obtained from a particular statue found in villages — a figure of a foreign goddess. When you find one, interact with it to exchange for a Vessel. Each Vessel increases your maximum stamina.

Spirit Orbs serve a similar function. Rumour has it that a second type of statue — one with a pair of horns — appears rarely in villages, underground, and even in the Nether. He offers suspicious bargains in exchange for currency.

## Gliding

If you acquire a paraglider, stamina governs glide duration. Running out of stamina mid-glide causes an immediate drop, so manage your pool carefully during long descents.
