# Movement

You are faster and more agile than you might expect. RAD3 includes an extended movement system through ParCool that goes well beyond vanilla jumping and sprinting.

Learning to move well pays dividends in both exploration and combat — getting around obstacles quickly, escaping bad situations, and reaching places most players cannot.
