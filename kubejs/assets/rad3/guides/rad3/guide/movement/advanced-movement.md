# Mounts and Late-Game Travel

Once you move past foot travel, RAD3 offers several options for faster, more interesting exploration.

## Horses

Horse behaviour has been significantly improved. Key changes:

- Horses have an inventory slot for a lead — no more losing your horse because you forgot to tie it up
- Horses only wander 16 blocks from where you dismounted
- Horses can swim
- Horses fit in boats for river and ocean crossings
- Additional quality-of-life improvements throughout

Early-game, a good horse is the most practical mount available. They handle RAD3's hilly terrain well and need no special fuel or maintenance.

## Dragons

Dragon Mounts: Legacy is included in the pack. Taming and riding a dragon is a significant late-game achievement — a reward for sustained progression rather than something you stumble into.

## Immersive Aircraft

The pack includes Immersive Aircraft, providing craftable flying vehicles for mid-to-late game aerial exploration. These consume stamina or fuel depending on the vehicle type.

## Small Ships

Water-based exploration is available through Small Ships. If your world involves significant ocean or river travel, these offer far faster transit than swimming or basic boats.

## Bonfires as a network

For point-to-point fast travel, your [Bonfire](../getting-started/bonfires.md) network remains the most reliable option regardless of where you are in progression. Build it consistently and it will always be faster than any mount for returning to known locations.
