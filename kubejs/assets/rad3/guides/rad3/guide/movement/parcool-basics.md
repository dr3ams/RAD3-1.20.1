# ParCool Basics

ParCool adds a set of movement abilities that are available from the moment you spawn. Most have default keybinds — check **Settings > Controls** and search for "parcool" to see the full list and rebind anything that conflicts.

## Core abilities

**Step Up** — Walk directly up full blocks without jumping. Enabled by default. Can be toggled with a keybind if you need to turn it off for precision placement.

**Crawl** — Press **C** (default) to enter crawl mode. Lets you move through 1-block-high gaps. Essential in dungeons with low ceilings.

**Double Jump** — Press jump a second time while airborne to gain extra height or horizontal distance. Costs stamina.

**Wall Jump** — While next to a wall, press jump to launch away from it — upward and outward. In a narrow vertical shaft, you can alternate wall jumps to climb significant heights.

**Break Fall** — While falling, press sneak just before impact. Reduces or eliminates fall damage. The timing window is generous — do not wait too long.

## Stamina costs

ParCool actions draw from your stamina pool. Double jumps, wall jumps, and similar active moves each cost stamina on use. Running out of stamina in a bad situation means losing access to these abilities.

Your [Nourishment tier](../nourishment/four-tiers.md) affects both how quickly stamina drains and how fast it recovers. At Starving, ParCool actions become expensive. At Peak, they are much more sustainable.

## More moves

ParCool includes more abilities beyond the basics above. Explore the full keybind list in Controls to find additional maneuvers like ledge grabs and momentum rolls.
