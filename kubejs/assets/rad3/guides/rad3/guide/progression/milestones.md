# Progression Milestones

Over 30 milestones are tracked automatically as you play. They measure your progress across the most significant events in RAD3 — boss kills, dimension visits, and acquisition of specific items.

## What milestones unlock

**Dynamic loot tables** — reaching specific milestones permanently alters what appears in dungeon chests and world loot. High-tier artifacts and rare materials only begin spawning once you have proven your worth by hitting the appropriate milestone threshold.

**Scaling difficulty** — your Game Stage, built from your milestone count, causes mobs across the world to gain better attributes and equipment. The challenge grows alongside the rewards.

## Key milestones to aim for early

While the full milestone list is tracked in the questbook, some significant ones to be aware of:

- Visiting the Nether
- Killing the Wither
- Reaching the End
- Defeating the Ender Dragon
- Visiting dimensions like the Undergarden, Aether, and Lands of Icaria

Each of these gates a tier of loot and difficulty progression.

## The chunk caveat

Loot tables only update in **newly generated chunks**. Areas you have already explored before hitting a milestone will not retroactively contain the updated loot. If you want tier-appropriate rewards, seek out terrain your world has not yet generated.

## Viewing your progress

Open the questbook and navigate to the Progression Milestones chapter to see which milestones you have hit and which rewards they unlocked.
