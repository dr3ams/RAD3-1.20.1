# Progression and XP

Experience is the primary currency in RAD3. It funds your skill tree, your enchantments, and your disenchanting — and because dying costs you your peak level's health bonus, losing a large XP pool to death has cascading consequences.

This section covers how to earn XP efficiently, how to protect it, and how the broader progression systems — the Passive Skill Tree and Milestones — interact with everything else.
