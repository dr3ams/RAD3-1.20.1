# Storing Your XP

When you die, XP stored in your level bar is lost. XP stored in an external vessel survives. Several storage options exist — choose based on what you have access to.

## XP Tomes

The most accessible early option. XP Tomes hold a limited amount of experience and are cheap to obtain. Carry one and fill it regularly during farming or dungeon runs. Multiple tomes can be carried for larger storage needs.

## Tome of Peritia

An upgraded XP Tome with unlimited storage capacity. Requires Blood Magic to craft — specifically, it requires blood. This makes it a mid-game option once you have started engaging with Blood Magic's systems.

## Experience Pump Upgrade (Recommended)

A backpack upgrade that automatically siphons XP into your Sophisticated Backpack. Requires the Tank Upgrade to be installed alongside it. Storage is limited by your backpack's tier and any Stack Upgrades installed.

This is the most ergonomic option for ongoing play — XP is captured passively without any manual intervention. A higher-tier backpack with Stack Upgrades provides substantial storage.

## Carved Nephrite

Found in geodes deep underground, Carved Nephrite is used to build Nephrite Siphons, Fountains, and Diodes — a physical world structure for storing experience. This is a larger infrastructure investment suited to established bases.

## Scrolls of Knowledge

Scrolls of Knowledge can store your entire XP bar at once. However, they are single-use, require a grave soul to craft, and force you to store the full amount — partial storage is not possible. Generally the least recommended option for routine use, but useful in emergencies.

## The rule of thumb

Store XP before any run you are not confident about. The cost of losing a large pool is compounding: fewer levels means fewer health hearts, which means the next run is more dangerous, which means more potential loss.
