# The Four Tiers

Your nourishment tier is evaluated every few seconds based on your current hunger and saturation values. When you cross a threshold, a message appears in chat. The tier adjusts your attributes automatically — no action required on your part.

## Starving — ZERO

Triggered when your hunger bar itself is nearly empty.

- Idle stamina regen heavily reduced
- Sprinting costs significantly more stamina
- Melee attacks cost more stamina
- Shield heart regeneration slowed

Entering a dungeon while Starving is dangerous. Every stamina-dependent action — combat rolls, parkour moves, blocking, sustained sprinting — drains faster and recovers slower. This is the state where fights become unwinnable quickly.

## Fed — LOW

Triggered when the hunger bar is full but saturation is empty or very low. This is the default state for players eating basic vanilla food.

- Minor combat roll cooldown improvement
- No stamina bonuses

Eating bread, raw meat, or other low-saturation food keeps you alive but does not make you effective. You are treading water.

## Well-Fed — MID

Triggered when saturation reaches a moderate threshold. Achievable with a single quality cooked meal from any Delight mod.

- Idle stamina regen improved
- Melee stamina cost reduced
- Maximum health increased by one bonus heart
- Combat roll recharge improved

A single good meal at the start of a session pushes you here and keeps you here for a meaningful stretch of activity.

## Peak Nourishment — HIGH

Triggered when saturation reaches a high threshold. Requires one of the better dishes from the Delight mods or a feast-tier item.

- Idle stamina regen significantly improved
- Sprint stamina cost reduced
- Melee stamina cost reduced
- Fastest combat roll recharge
- Bonus shield heart granted

Peak is the target state before any serious dungeon run. Saturation drains from activity, not from time — a Peak meal eaten before entering a dungeon will last through most of a run.
