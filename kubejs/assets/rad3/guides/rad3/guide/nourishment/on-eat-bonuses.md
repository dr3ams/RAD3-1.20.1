# On-Eat Bonuses

Every food registered in the nourishment system grants bonuses the moment it is eaten, separate from the passive tier effects. These apply regardless of what tier you are currently in.

## Fixed potion effect

Each registered dish has a thematically matched potion effect applied immediately on eating. The effect and its duration are shown in the item tooltip.

Some examples by food type:

- Meat dishes → Strength
- Seafood → Water Breathing or Dolphin's Grace
- Cave foods (Miner's Delight) → Night Vision
- Nether foods → Fire Resistance or related effects

Cup variants from Miner's Delight apply effects with shorter durations than full meals — they are designed as quick field options, not long-duration buffs.

## Random XP

A small amount of experience is awarded every time you eat a registered meal. The exact amount varies each time within a per-dish range. Over the course of a long play session, this adds up meaningfully.

## Random bonus effect

Dishes marked with **✦** in their tooltip have a chance to roll an additional random effect from a weighted pool on eating. When a bonus effect triggers, the message **✦ Inspired!** appears in your action bar.

Common bonus rolls include Speed, Haste, Jump Boost, and Regeneration.

Rare bonus rolls include Haste II, Strength II, and Fire Resistance.

The roll happens automatically — there is nothing special you need to do to trigger it. It simply fires with a random chance each time you eat a ✦-marked dish.

## Reading tooltips

Before cooking, check what a dish offers:

- The fixed effect and its duration
- The XP range
- Whether a ✦ bonus roll is possible

Not every dish has all three. Simple items may only provide one or two of these bonuses.
