# What to Eat

Four mod families have their food items fully registered in the nourishment system. Items from other mods still function as food but do not grant on-eat bonuses and provide less saturation.

## Registered food mods

**Farmer's Delight** is the primary food source. Full coverage across stews, pastas, soups, pies, sandwiches, and roasts. A quality Farmer's Delight meal is the most accessible route to Well-Fed or Peak tier.

**Miner's Delight** covers cave-themed soups and cup variants. The cup variants are intentionally designed as emergency rations — they are fast to eat mid-combat, provide short-duration effects, and are convenient to carry. They will not push you into Well-Fed tier on their own. Bring proper meals and use cups as backup.

**Ocean's Delight** covers Elder Guardian dishes and Guardian Soup. These are mid-to-late game foods tied to ocean monument content.

**Nether's Delight** covers Hoglin dishes and Grilled Strider. These become available once you are working in the Nether and provide effects suited to that environment.

## Which tier does each food reach?

There is no universal rule — saturation values vary by dish. As a general guide:

- Simple cooked meats (vanilla) → Fed (LOW) at best
- Basic Farmer's Delight dishes (sandwiches, simple soups) → Well-Fed (MID)
- Substantial Farmer's Delight meals (roasts, complex stews) → Peak (HIGH)
- Cup variants from Miner's Delight → Fed (LOW), high-speed application

Check item tooltips for each dish's fixed effect, XP range, and bonus roll eligibility before committing to a cooking session.

## Tips

- Eat a Peak-tier meal immediately before entering a dungeon. It will outlast most runs.
- Keep Miner's Delight cups in your hotbar for in-combat emergencies. They are not a replacement for a real meal.
- Tooltips marked with ✦ indicate the dish has a chance to roll a bonus effect on eat.
