# Hunger vs. Saturation

Minecraft tracks two food-related values simultaneously. Most players only notice one of them.

## Hunger

Hunger is the visible bar — the drumstick icons in your HUD. It drops when you are active and is what causes starvation damage when it empties. Any food item refills it instantly, regardless of quality.

**Hunger alone determines nothing in the nourishment system** except whether you are Starving.

## Saturation

Saturation is an invisible buffer that drains *before* the hunger bar moves at all. When you eat, most of the value goes into saturation first. Only when saturation reaches zero does the visible hunger bar start dropping.

Cheap food like bread, raw meat, and most vanilla items provide very little saturation. Quality cooked meals from the Delight mods provide large amounts — enough to keep your saturation high through sustained activity.

## Why this matters

You can have a completely full hunger bar while having **zero saturation**. In that state, you receive no nourishment bonuses whatsoever. A full bar does not mean Well-Fed.

Players who eat bread to stay alive will sit in the Fed (LOW) tier — alive, but without any of the stamina, health, or combat bonuses that proper food provides.

## The practical takeaway

Watch your tier indicator in chat, not your hunger bar. The tier tells you what your body is actually doing. If you are Fed but not Well-Fed, the solution is not eating more bread — it is eating a quality meal.
