# Hunger and Nourishment

RAD3 includes a custom hunger overhaul. What you eat — and how well — directly modifies your stamina, combat effectiveness, and survivability.

The most important concept to understand first: a full hunger bar does not mean you are well-fed. The game tracks two separate values, and the visible bar only tells half the story.
