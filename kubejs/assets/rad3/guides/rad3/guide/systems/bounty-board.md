# Bounty Board

Bounty Boards generate in villages and provide a rotating selection of contracts — tasks that ask you to collect items, hunt specific mobs, or craft goods in exchange for valuable rewards.

## How to use one

1. Find a Bounty Board in a village (it appears as a noticeboard-style block)
2. Right-click to see available contracts
3. Take a bounty into your inventory
4. Fulfil the requirements listed on the bounty
5. Return to any Bounty Board to claim your reward

Bounties refresh over time, so check back regularly even if the current selection does not suit you.

## Why bother

Bounty rewards scale with the difficulty of the task and can include:

- Coins usable in the questbook Market
- Useful materials and consumables
- XP and other progression resources

For players who do not enjoy grinding mobs or mining in isolation, Bounty Boards provide structured goals with guaranteed rewards. They are particularly useful in the early-to-mid game when consistent income is harder to establish.

## Tips

- Carry multiple bounties at once to complete several on a single dungeon run
- Mob-hunting bounties often align naturally with dungeon exploration
- Check JEI if you are unsure how to craft or find a required item
