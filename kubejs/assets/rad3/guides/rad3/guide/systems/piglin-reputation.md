# Piglin Reputation

Piglins now have a reputation system that works similarly to Villagers. Your standing with them changes based on your actions, and high or low reputation has significant practical consequences.

## Raising reputation

- Fighting alongside Piglins against Wither-type mobs
- Giving Piglins food (right-click them with porkchops or cooked porkchops)
- Bartering with them regularly
- General positive interaction

## Lowering reputation

- Attacking or killing Piglins or Piglin Brutes
- Mining gold near Piglins without authorisation
- Opening chests near Piglins without authorisation

## Reputation thresholds

| Reputation | Effect |
|---|---|
| +100 | Authorised to open chests and mine gold nearby |
| +50 | No longer required to wear gold armour around them |
| 0 | Default state — wear gold armour or be attacked |
| -50 | Piglins refuse to barter with you |
| -100 | Attacked on sight regardless of gold armour |

## Bartering improvements

Piglins now accept gold nuggets and gold blocks in addition to ingots:

- **Gold nuggets** — higher chance of common barter items; stack sizes halved
- **Gold blocks** — higher chance of rare barter items; stack sizes doubled

## Piglin behaviour

Piglins seek out and eat food items, particularly porkchops. Feeding a Piglin restores their health in an amount equal to the food's hunger value. Piglins now shake their heads when declining an offered item and emit particles or sounds when accepting or refusing.
