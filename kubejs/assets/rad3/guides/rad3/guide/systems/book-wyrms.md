# Book Wyrms

Book Wyrms are small lizards that spawn in forests and come in seven colours. Each colour has an affinity for a specific category of enchantments. Feed them unwanted enchanted books and they will digest those books over time, eventually producing new random enchanted books from their colour's pool.

## The seven colours

| Colour | Enchantment pool |
|---|---|
| Gray | Any enchantment — the most common colour |
| Red | Melee weapons (swords, axes) |
| Orange | Ranged weapons (bows, crossbows) |
| Green | Tools (pickaxes, shovels, axes) |
| Blue | Armour and shields |
| Teal | Fishing rods and tridents |
| Purple | Miscellaneous modded items |

To recycle enchanted books, simply feed the appropriate Wyrm by right-clicking it with the book. The Wyrm digests the book over time and eventually produces a new enchanted book from its pool.

## Wyrm stats and genes

Each Wyrm has three hidden stats that affect performance:

- **Enchanting level** — determines how many levels the Wyrm needs to digest before producing a new book, and how powerful the enchantments it can output are
- **Digesting speed** — how quickly the Wyrm processes books
- **Indigestion chance** — how likely the Wyrm is to regurgitate partially-digested material (shredded paper and magical fluids) instead of a proper book

These stats are genetic and passed to offspring. Use the **Book Wyrm Analyzer** (check JEI for the recipe) to inspect a Wyrm's stats. Breed strategically to improve your stock over generations.

## Indigestion and Wyrmutagen

Indigested output — shredded paper and digestive fluids — can be re-fed to a Wyrm to recover part of the lost magic, or processed into **Wyrmutagen**. Wyrmutagen lets you directly modify the gene values of a Wyrm's offspring, giving much finer control over breeding outcomes than natural selection alone.

## Breeding

Feed two Wyrms plain books to breed them. Offspring inherit a blend of parental genes with some random variance. With Wyrmutagen, you can push offspring stats in a specific direction.

A well-bred, high-level Blue Wyrm fed a constant supply of unwanted armour enchantments becomes an efficient source of high-tier armour enchanted books — a meaningful mid-to-late game crafting resource.
