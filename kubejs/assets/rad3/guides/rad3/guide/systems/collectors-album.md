# Collector's Album

The Collector's Album is a passive progression system built around 900 collectible cards. Cards drop from hostile mobs in card packages, which you open to reveal five random cards. Slotting cards into your Album unlocks passive bonuses as your collection grows.

## Getting started

Craft the Album (check JEI for the recipe). From then on, hostile mobs have a chance to drop card packages when killed. Open a package to receive five cards of varying rarity.

## The five categories

Cards are sorted into five categories in the Album:

- Tools
- Armour
- Mobs
- Nature
- Items

Each card has a category, a rarity tier, and a point value based on that rarity. Cards slot into their designated position in the Album by card number.

## Rarity tiers

From weakest to strongest: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Mythical

Higher rarity cards are harder to obtain and worth more points.

## Bonuses

As your total point value climbs and as you fill complete categories, you unlock passive effects. These include:

- Resistance
- Regeneration
- Extra health
- Other attribute bonuses

The specific thresholds and their rewards are visible in the Album interface.

## Tips

- Kill lots of different mob types — different mobs appear to have different card pools
- Duplicate cards are inevitable; there is no exchange system, so focus on accumulating points rather than completing specific entries
- Category completion bonuses are significant — prioritise filling one category at a time if you want efficient bonus unlocks
