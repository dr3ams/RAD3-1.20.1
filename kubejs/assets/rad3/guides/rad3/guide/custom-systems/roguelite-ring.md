# Roguelite Ring

The Roguelite Ring is a curio item that grows in power through use. Unlike static gear, it gains strength each time you clear a dungeon run — making it a long-term investment that rewards consistent Dungeon Raid engagement.

## Equipping

Place the Roguelite Ring in a curio ring slot (open the curio interface with **Alt+E** or via the button in your inventory screen).

## Passive Stats

While equipped, the ring provides attribute bonuses that scale with its current level. Base stats include:

- Increased attack damage
- Minor health bonus
- Run-specific bonuses that activate inside the dungeon dimension

The exact values increase each time the ring levels up.

## Leveling

The ring gains XP from **Dungeon Raid** activity while it is equipped during a run:
- Mob kills inside the dungeon contribute ring XP
- Chest opens contribute ring XP
- Surviving a full run without dying gives a completion bonus

Ring XP is separate from your personal Raid XP and rank. The ring levels independently on its own progression track.

## Level Cap and Upgrades

The ring has a maximum level. At certain level thresholds, new passive effects activate in addition to the scaling stat bonuses. Reaching the cap produces a fully leveled ring that remains at peak power indefinitely.

The ring **does not reset on your death** — its progression is stored in the item's NBT and persists. Losing the ring (dropping it, having it destroyed) does reset it, so protect it accordingly.

## Tips

- Equip the ring before every Dungeon Raid, even early on — early-rank runs still contribute leveling XP
- Do not unequip and re-equip mid-run; equip it at your base before entering
- The ring's power at high levels makes it one of the most impactful single curio slots in the game — prioritise leveling it early
