# Exploration Overhaul

The Exploration Overhaul is a set of changes to how the world generates and how structures are distributed. Together they make exploration more deliberate and more rewarding.

## Structure Density

Structure spawn rates have been tuned across the board. Dungeons, villages, ruins, and other points of interest appear more frequently than in vanilla but are spread more intentionally — you will not find three villages overlapping, but you will encounter structures more regularly as you travel.

Rare structures remain rare. High-value locations like Dimensional Dungeons and boss arenas are not affected by density increases.

## Dimension Gating

Several structures only generate in specific dimensions or biomes as intended by their mod, with no additional spreading into vanilla overworld contexts. This keeps dimensional content feeling like a reason to explore those dimensions rather than something you stumble on by accident.

## Lootr Integration

All dungeon chest loot uses the [Lootr system](../world/dungeon-treasures.md) — per-player individual rolls, no shared depletion. This applies to every structure in the pack regardless of which mod added it.

## Exploration and Milestones

Exploring new terrain and visiting new structures contributes to your [Progression Milestones](../progression/milestones.md). Many milestones are triggered by reaching specific locations or dimensions for the first time.

**Loot only updates in freshly generated chunks.** If you want milestone-tier loot, explore areas your world has not generated yet. Use Xaero's World Map to identify explored vs unexplored territory.

## Xaero's Integration

Xaero's Minimap and World Map are fully integrated. Structure waypoints are auto-added when you discover major locations. The fair-play edition of the minimap is used — cave mapping and entity radar are limited to keep exploration meaningful.
