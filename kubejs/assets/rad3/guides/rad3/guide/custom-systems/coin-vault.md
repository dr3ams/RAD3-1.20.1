# Coin Vault

The Coin Vault is RAD3's currency system. Differebt coins are earned from quests, bounties, and other sources, and spent at the **Market** — a quest-based shop system — on items, materials, and services not easily obtainable elsewhere.

## Earning Coins

- **Quest rewards** — the primary source; most quests award coins alongside other rewards
- **Bounty Board completions** — see [Bounty Board](../systems/bounty-board.md)
- **Dungeon loot** — including special Dimensional Coins.
- **Raid Tokens** — Raid Tokens earned from the [Dungeon Raid](dungeon-raid.md) system can be exchanged for coins at specific conversion rates

## The Vault

Like Raid Tokens, coins can be stored in a **virtual vault** rather than carried as physical items.

| Command | Description |
|---|---|
| `/coinvault` | Show your current coin balance |
| `/coinvault withdraw <amount>` | Move coins to your inventory as physical coin items |
| `/coinvault deposit` | Deposit held coin items back into the vault |

Physical coin items can also be carried normally and used directly at the Market.

## The Market

The Market is accessed through the questbook. Navigate to the Market chapter to see available purchases. Stock includes:

- Healing items and consumables
- Rare crafting materials
- Convenience items and utility goods
- Items that are otherwise difficult to obtain without specific progression

Market stock may expand as the modpack updates. Check back regularly.

## Tips

- Store coins in the vault rather than your inventory to avoid losing them on death (physical coin items drop with your other loot at your tombstone, but vault balance is always safe)
- Bounty Boards are the most efficient early-game coin source when you are not yet doing Dungeon Raids
- Some Market items are priced to be aspirational — save up rather than spending everything immediately
