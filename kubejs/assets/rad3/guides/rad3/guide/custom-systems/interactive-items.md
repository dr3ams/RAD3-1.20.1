# Interactive Items

Interactive Items are a category of custom right-click items — each one does something unique when used. They are found in loot, crafted, or rewarded through quests. This page covers the ones currently in the system.

## Guard Summon

Right-click to summon a **Personal Guard** — a Guard Villager NPC that follows you, defends you from hostile mobs, and persists for **1 minute** before fading.

- The guard targets any mob that attacks you within aggro range
- It teleports to you if it falls too far behind
- You can only have one active guard at a time — trying to summon a second while one is active does nothing
- Single use — the item is consumed on use

## Buzzing Branch

Right-click to release **3 Guardian Bees** that orbit you and attack any mob that strikes you within range. The swarm lasts **1 minute**.

- Bees are immune to losing their stinger — they can sting repeatedly without dying
- The swarm teleports to you if bees stray too far
- The Buzzing Branch is a **durable item**, not single use — each activation applies durability damage. When durability runs out, the branch shatters
- Has a cooldown between uses

## Roguelite Ring (Active)

See the [Roguelite Ring](roguelite-ring.md) page — it is both a stat item and an interactive item with its own dedicated mechanics.

## Tips

- Guard Summon and Buzzing Branch are best used at the start of a difficult encounter, not mid-fight when you are already taking heavy damage
- The Buzzing Branch's durability means you can bring it on many runs — treat it as reusable equipment rather than a one-off consumable
- Check JEI for any additional interactive items added in later updates
