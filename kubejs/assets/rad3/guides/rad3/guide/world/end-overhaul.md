# The End

The End in RAD3 has been significantly overhauled in both layout and difficulty. Do not approach it with vanilla expectations.

## End Island — YUNG's Better End Island

The spawn island has been redesigned. Obsidian pillars, End gateways, the spawn platform, and the central portal structure all look and function differently.

**The dragon does not spawn immediately.** Instead, it is summoned when a player approaches the bell tower at the centre of the island. Be ready before you move in.

**Resummoning the dragon** uses a different crystal placement. Rather than the four positions adjacent to the portal, you will see four bedrock blocks on the floor inside the tower — one in each directional wing. Place crystals on these blocks to begin resummoning. Standard vanilla crystal positions still work if you prefer the original method.

## End terrain — Nullscape

Beyond the spawn island, the End's terrain has been completely replaced by Nullscape. The dimension now reaches 384 blocks in height and generates alien terrain at a scale designed to make you feel small. Different regions have distinct terrain properties — porous sponge-like formations, stretched landscapes, stacked rounded islands.

Exploration in the End is genuinely rewarding and genuinely disorienting. Bring a map and mark your return portal.

## The Dragon fight

The Savage Ender Dragon overhaul makes the fight meaningfully harder:

- Scales with the number of players present
- Scales with world difficulty settings
- **Immune to explosion-based tactics** — bed cheese does not work
- Uses lightning attacks
- Responds to flight with anti-air counterattacks — staying airborne is not a safe strategy
- End crystals respawn during the fight

Prepare thoroughly. Bring your best gear, a full health pool, Peak-tier food, and enough healing items for a sustained fight.
