# Chunk Claiming

RAD3 includes chunk claiming through **Open Parties and Claims**, accessible via the world map. Claimed chunks protect your builds and infrastructure from other players on multiplayer servers.

## Claiming a chunk

1. Open the world map with **M**
2. Right-click the chunk you want to claim
3. Select **Claim Selected** from the dropdown

To claim multiple chunks at once, hold right-click and drag your cursor across the chunks you want to include.

On a server, you can also **forceload** an already-claimed chunk by clicking it and selecting Forceload Selected. Forceloaded chunks remain active even when no players are nearby — useful for automated systems and farms.

## Teams and Parties

RAD3 uses two separate team systems with different purposes. It is important to understand the distinction:

**FTB Teams** (created with `/ftbteams party create [name]`) — ties players together for quest completion. Members of the same FTB Team share quest progress, meaning the whole group advances together rather than tracking separately.

**Open Parties** (created with `/openpac-parties create`) — governs chunk claiming permissions. Party members can build and interact within each other's claimed chunks. This is the system that controls who has access to your protected land.

For cooperative play, you will typically want both: an FTB Team for shared quest progression and an Open Party for shared land permissions.
