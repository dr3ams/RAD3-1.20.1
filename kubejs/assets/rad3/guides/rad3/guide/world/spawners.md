# Spawners

Spawners in RAD3 behave differently from vanilla in two important ways. Both changes are designed to make dungeons more dynamic and prevent indefinite mob farms.

## They are much harder to break

Breaking a spawner requires **20 seconds** with an iron pickaxe. You cannot rush it. A spawner in an active dungeon room is a serious threat to anyone trying to break it mid-fight.

**Light the room first.** Torches placed around the spawner prevent it from activating while you work. The questbook rewards you with a stack of torches early specifically for this reason. Get into the habit of lighting spawner rooms before engaging the spawner itself.

## They burn out

Each spawner can only spawn **20 mobs** before it burns out and stops working permanently. This has two implications:

**In your favour:** A spawner you are fighting through will eventually stop. If you are struggling to clear a room, knowing the spawner will exhaust itself gives you a fallback — take damage, retreat, let it burn through its pool, then return to a static fight.

**Against you:** Spawner farms built around RAD3 spawners will not last. After 20 spawns, the spawner is dead. Do not invest in farming infrastructure built around a specific spawner unless you plan to replace it.

## Spawner Extraction

The custom Spawner Extraction system allows you to capture spawners under specific conditions. Check the questbook's custom features section for details on how that works.
