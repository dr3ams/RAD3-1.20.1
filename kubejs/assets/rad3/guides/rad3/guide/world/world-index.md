# The World

RAD3's world is larger, more dangerous, and more structured than vanilla Minecraft. Dungeons are more numerous, more difficult, and more rewarding. Familiar systems like spawners and chests work differently than you expect.

This section covers the world-level systems every player needs to understand before venturing into structured content.
