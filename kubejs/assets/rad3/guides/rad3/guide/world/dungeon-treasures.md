# Dungeon Treasures

Dungeon loot in RAD3 uses the **Lootr** system. Every chest, barrel, and shulker box found in a generated structure provides **individual loot rolls per player** rather than shared loot.

## What this means

In a standard Minecraft dungeon, one player opens a chest and the loot is gone for everyone else. With Lootr, each player who opens a chest gets their own independent loot roll. A chest opened by a group of four players generates four separate loot pools — one for each person.

This makes cooperative dungeon exploration significantly more rewarding. There is no competition for chest contents.

## Identifying Lootr chests

Lootr chests appear as **bejeweled or decorated chests** rather than the plain wooden appearance of normal Minecraft chests. Once you have opened a Lootr chest, it changes to a **bluish-grey colour**, indicating you have already collected your loot from it. Other players may not have opened it yet and it will still appear normal to them.

Lootr also applies to barrels and shulker boxes in some structures.

## Loot quality and milestones

What appears inside Lootr chests scales with your [Progression Milestones](../progression/milestones.md). High-tier materials and rare artifacts only begin appearing once you have hit the appropriate milestone thresholds.

**Old chunks will not update.** If you explored an area before hitting a milestone, those chests will not retroactively contain better loot. Seek fresh terrain for milestone-appropriate rewards.
