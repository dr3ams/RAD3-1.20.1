# No Natural Healing

Natural regeneration from food has been **disabled** in RAD3. Eating until your hunger bar is full will not restore a single heart. This is the single most important difference from vanilla Minecraft and the one most likely to get new players killed.

## How to heal

Healing requires dedicated items. You will need to find, craft, or buy them.

| Item | Uses | HP per use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plaster | 4 | 0.5 hearts | Easiest to find early |
| Healing Salve | 4 | 0.5 hearts | Similar to Plaster |
| Bandage | 6 | 0.5 hearts | Easy to craft, better value |
| Medkit | 16 | 1 heart | Slow to use, requires crafting |
| Enchanted Medkit | 16 | 1.5 hearts | Grants Absorption; requires Ghast Tear |
| Estus Flask | 3+ uses | variable | Upgradeable with Estus Shards |

Regeneration potions, golden apples, and items with the Regeneration effect also work. Search for healing items in JEI if you are unsure what you have access to.

## Where to get early healing

Plasters are rewarded from your first quest in the questbook. Bandages are cheap to craft and should be your default healing item through the early game. Keep several in your inventory at all times — running out mid-dungeon is fatal.

## Healing from other dimensions

The Aether dimension provides Healing Stones. The Lands of Icaria provides Healing Flasks. These are mid-to-late game options that become available once you have access to those dimensions.
