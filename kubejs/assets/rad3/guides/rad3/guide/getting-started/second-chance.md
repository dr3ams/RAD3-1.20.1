# Second Chance

If you have **7 or more hearts**, any single hit that would have killed you outright will instead leave you at half a heart.

This only applies to one-shot kills — damage that, in a single instance, would have taken you from alive to dead. It does not prevent death from sustained damage over multiple hits.

## What this means in practice

In the early game with only 6 hearts, Second Chance does not apply. You can be killed by a single strong hit with no safety net.

Once you have invested in Level Hearts and your pool is above 7, you gain a meaningful buffer against sudden deaths. A creeper explosion, a critical hit from a tough dungeon mob, a fall — these are survivable if your health pool is large enough.

This is one of the strongest arguments for prioritising XP early: the health you gain from leveling is not just more hit points, it is the threshold that activates this safety mechanic.

## It is not a totem

Second Chance does not save you from a second hit. If something one-shots you down to half a heart and hits you again before you can react, you die. Use the moment it gives you to disengage, not to keep fighting.
