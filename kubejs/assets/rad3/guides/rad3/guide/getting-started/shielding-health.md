# Shielding Health

Beyond your red hearts, you have a pool of gold absorption hearts. This is your shield — a regenerating buffer that sits in front of your real health.

## How it works

Shield hearts absorb incoming damage before your red hearts take any. When you are hit, the shield depletes first. Once the shield is gone, red hearts take damage normally.

The shield regenerates automatically whenever you spend time out of active combat. Step back, avoid hits for a moment, and you will see the gold hearts refill. This makes tactical retreating genuinely valuable — a disengaged player is a recovering player.

## Power Tokens and Shield Power

Power Tokens drop from mobs and appear in dungeon loot. They can be crafted into **Shield Power**, a consumable that does two things:

1. Gives you a temporary boost to your current shield
2. Permanently increases your maximum shield capacity by one heart

The permanent increase is lost when you die. This creates a meaningful mid-run investment: spending tokens increases your survivability for the current session, but you lose the upgrade if you do not make it out.

## Nourishment and your shield

Your [Nourishment tier](../nourishment/four-tiers.md) affects shield performance directly:

- **Peak (HIGH)** — grants a bonus shield heart and the fastest shield regen rate
- **Starving (ZERO)** — shield regen is significantly slowed

Eat well before dungeon runs. The shield bonus from Peak tier stacks with any Power Token upgrades you have active.
