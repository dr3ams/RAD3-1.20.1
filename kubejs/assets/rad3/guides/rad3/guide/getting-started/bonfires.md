# Bonfires

Bonfires are craftable fast-travel points that also set your spawn location. They are RAD3's equivalent of a waypoint system, but you have to earn each one by building it yourself.

## Crafting a Bonfire

You need two components:

- **Coiled Sword** — functions as both a melee weapon and the anchor of the Bonfire structure. It sets mobs on fire on hit.
- **Pile of Bone and Ash** — Piles of Ash have a chance to drop from mobs that are killed by fire. Combine with bones to create the pile.

Place the Pile of Bone and Ash on the ground, then right-click it with the Coiled Sword. This embeds the sword in the pile and creates an unlit Bonfire. Right-click the Bonfire to light it.

## Using Bonfires

Right-clicking a lit Bonfire opens the fast-travel menu. You can name your Bonfire for easy identification and travel to any other Bonfire you have previously lit. Lighting a Bonfire also sets it as your spawn point — dying will respawn you there.
