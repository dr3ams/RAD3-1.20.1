# Your Health Bar

Your health bar in RAD3 has three distinct layers. Understanding all three is essential to reading your actual survivability at a glance.

## Base hearts

You start with 6 hearts (12 HP). This does not change unless you actively invest in increasing it.

## Level Hearts

Your maximum health scales with experience. As your XP level climbs, you gain bonus hearts — up to a meaningful increase over your base pool.

The key rule: only your **peak level since your last death** determines how many bonus hearts you have. If you are currently level 20 but your peak was level 35, you still benefit from the 35-level bonus. Spending or storing your XP does not reduce your hearts. However, if you die, your peak resets to 0 and your bonus hearts disappear until you rebuild your levels.

This means dying has a compounding cost: you lose your XP progress *and* your health advantage for the next run.

## Shielding Health

Beyond your red hearts, you have a pool of absorption hearts shown in gold. This is your shield — a buffer that regenerates automatically when you spend time out of combat.

Shield hearts do not require any healing items. Step away from enemies, wait a moment, and they return. Use this to your advantage: disengage, let your shield recover, then re-engage.

Power Tokens, which drop from mobs and appear in loot, can be used to craft Shield Power — a consumable that temporarily boosts your shield and permanently increases your maximum shield by one heart. This bonus is lost on death, so save your tokens for runs you intend to finish.
